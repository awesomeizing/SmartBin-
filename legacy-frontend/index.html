// the problem

Trucks that visit bins nobody filled

Municipal collection runs on fixed schedules that bear no relationship to what is actually in the bins. Commercial districts overflow before their scheduled visit. Residential bins get emptied at 20% full. The result wastes fuel, inflates costs, and still leaves streets dirtier than they should be.
